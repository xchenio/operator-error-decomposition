## Matched schedule comparison at 1000 functions

With the same pretrained trunks, zero residual initialization, batch 32 and 3000 updates:

| Trunk | Constant LR validation relative L2 | Cosine validation relative L2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| MLP | 0.389815 ± 0.003031 | 0.374807 ± 0.010134 |
| Fourier | 0.431010 ± 0.006794 | 0.412541 ± 0.008236 |

Cosine improves mean-relative-L2 in all ten paired seeds. RMSE improves in four of five
MLP and all five Fourier seeds. Neither family reaches the training projection-excess
threshold: cosine mean excess is 0.079462 (MLP) and 0.137961 (Fourier).
MLP's cosine mean is still slightly worse than the input-mean baseline 0.374509.

This differs from the n=128 small-batch result, where decay worsened validation in all
paired seeds. The defensible interpretation is a sample-count/budget interaction within
this dataset. Equal updates at different training sizes change average reuse per function;
this is not isolated causal evidence for data volume alone. Nor does it contradict the
core finding that better training fit does not guarantee better validation.

## Training-only POD plus linear coefficient regression

We construct uncentered residual POD from training targets minus each input's spatial
mean. Input statistics use training data only. Mean-loss ridge regression includes an
unpenalized intercept. Ranks 16/32/64 and six alphas from 1e-4 to 10 are all reported;
selection uses validation mean relative L2.

The selected rank16/alpha10 model scores 0.373656 on validation, versus baseline 0.374509,
an absolute difference of only 0.000853 after 18-candidate validation selection. Alpha10
is the grid boundary. This is not a confirmed test improvement or an optimally tuned
global solution. Its validation projection oracle is 0.108982; rank64 lowers the oracle
to 0.036648 without improving the selected linear predictor. A nonlinear coefficient
control on the identical POD basis is therefore the next discriminating experiment.

POD ridge is not a faithful implementation of the complete POD-DeepONet or two-step
training methods. Those references and a second PDE remain missing. Results, identities
and case-level records are in public_scale1000_v1 and public_pod_ridge_v1.
See [the full follow-up protocol and limitations](../docs/PUBLIC_SCALE1000_V1.md).
